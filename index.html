Act 3 — Reality check: noise, split-brain and what hardware would need

Real hardware is noisy. With visibility V, P_win = ½ + V·√2/4: the quantum strategy stops beating 75 % at V = 1/√2 ≈ 0.707. For leader election P(exactly one leader) = V + 3(1−V)/8; the failures are split-brain (two leaders) and no leader. Across three runs on IonQ's Forte 1 noise model we measured 96.0–97.0 % — roughly one election in 30 to 40 produces two leaders. Engineering requirements: entanglement must survive in memory for at least the decision window (tens of ms), and entanglement distribution rate must match decision rate. None of this exists at scale today.
